State and timing failures overlap; do not add their counts. The data's operand
distribution and the adapter's explicitly unobserved behaviors make these
counts unsuitable as an emulator accuracy percentage.

Readable cases: 21 pass and 7 fail per decoder. The failures expose MULS zero
flags, DIVS quotient/remainder packing, ORI reserved status bits (CCR and SR),
STOP PC advance, TRAP exception handling, and TAS register preservation.
The legacy suite has 17 checked groups and 67 unfinished templates reported SKIP.

The fixed CI sample executes 1,028 cases per decoder: 500 pass, 528 fail, and
16 additional upstream cases are quarantined. Its checked-in per-bit baseline
allows the reviewed current failures while rejecting new ones. Full audit
reports are generated locally under build/conformance-full/; CI publishes the
sample and readable-case reports as artifacts. Neither gate success nor these
counts mean the CPU is complete.
